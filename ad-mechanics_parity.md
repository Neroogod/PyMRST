# ad-mechanics parity ledger

## Scope and declaration

This ledger compares MRST 2024a `modules/ad-mechanics/` with
`pymrst/modules/ad_mechanics/`, as certified on 2026-08-01. Behavioral parity
means that the Python owner, public contract, numerical path and focused
validation evidence exist; it does not mean byte-for-byte MATLAB figure output.

The declaration gates are satisfied. The machine-readable source of truth is
`docs/ad_mechanics/parity.csv`.

## Key numerical certification

- `poroParams`: all 30 elastic pairs, three complete bases, and 371 individual
  relation inversions generated by local MATLAB/MRST.
- `setupOperatorsVEM`: direct MATLAB stiffness, RHS, strain, stress and
  Dirichlet comparison, including custom stabilization and nonzero body load.
- `equationsPoroMechanics`: residual and both displacement/pressure Jacobian
  blocks agree with MATLAB to machine precision.
- Fixed stress: regenerated MATLAB reports compare iteration topology, cut
  counts, mechanics residuals and nested nonlinear histories for water,
  oil-water and black-oil; rollback and restart branches are tested.
- Mechanics edge cases: moving prescribed displacement, initial stress,
  external/body loads, custom VEM stabilization and general polyhedral grids.
- Objectives: direct MATLAB fixtures certify uplift values and AD derivatives;
  the adjoint gradient also agrees with central finite differences.
- Plotting: field-history contracts are headless-tested and the shared plotting
  suite contains a renderer-safe off-screen VTK screenshot regression.

## Analytical examples

Mandel exposes dimensional/time-normalized pressure histories, displacement,
analytic profiles and error histories. Terzaghi implements implicit
cell-centered consolidation alongside the analytic pressure and degree-of-
consolidation histories. Their schemas and error bounds are regression-tested.

## MATLAB-backed workflow matrix

The six 2D combinations—water, oil-water and black-oil, each fully coupled and
fixed stress—retain MATLAB state and report fixtures. Comparisons include
pressure, phase state, displacement, strain, stress, wells and convergence
reports. Mini-Norne has the corresponding six-case, 21-step MATLAB history
matrix. The documented black-oil producer `qOs` report-zero convention remains
the only quantity-specific comparison exception; reports 1–20 pass the 2%
rate tolerance (observed maximum 1.57%).
